## Notes for the wake-partners ingestion

1. **Partner names are NOT all unique candidates for the `partners` table** — many will be duplicates of existing partners (e.g., "Pagar.me" likely already exists). De-dup against the production `partners` table before insert.
2. **`partner_type` mapping** to existing schema (`tech_partner` | `agency_partner`):
   - Sections §1 (Agências) → `agency_partner`
   - Sections §2, §4, §5, §6, §7 → `tech_partner`
   - Section §3 (Marketplace) → `tech_partner` (Anymarket is the canonical entry)
3. **The KB articles themselves** (672 in `docs/kb-delivery-batch-*.md` + 7 in `docs/kb-priority-*.md`) carry the content — capabilities, pricing, contacts, troubleshooting — that should enrich `partners.long_description`, `support_details`, or feed into a new `partner_knowledge_articles` table.
4. **Verify before publishing**: every partner here was mentioned in WhatsApp at least once. Some may be defunct, paused, or never went beyond a POC. Cross-check active status against `Partner Ops` before publishing externally.
