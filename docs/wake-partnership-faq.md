# Wake Partnership Program — FAQ

Extracted from WAKE | Time Parcerias, Comercial Cross WAKE, and ANYMARKET & WAKE groups.

---

## Partnership Program

**What are the partnership tiers?**
Wake has partner tiers — the highest is **Elite**. Tier affects visibility in events, panel invites, and incentive programs. Elite partners get priority placement at events like ECBR and Wake Evolution.

**How does a new partner get officially registered?**
All new partners must be listed in the official partners spreadsheet (the base that feeds the PRM). Leads and prospects being negotiated in Salesforce must also be migrated there. The process runs through a form that will be absorbed by the PRM.

**Does Wake provide a badge or seal for agency partners?**
Yes. A partner badge is sent after the Kickoff. Partners can use it on their institutional site and marketing materials. Any usage should be validated with Wake's marketing team (Math Coneglian can orient).

**What is the onboarding structure for new partners?**
- **Onboarding phase:** 4 weeks, 1 hour per week
- **Ongoing:** 1 hour per month
Partners can be segmented by tier. Onboarding sessions are run by the Parcerias team.

**What are Co-Sell Acceleration and Co-Build Sessions?**
Two recurring rituals launched to strengthen Wake + Partner collaboration:
- **Co-Sell Acceleration** (Fridays 9h–10h): For SDRs, account executives, pre-sales and commercial partners. Focus on pipeline qualification, discovery, and conversion.
- **Co-Build Sessions** (Fridays 10h–11h): For solution architects, pre-sales, technical specialists. Focus on solution architecture, integrations, and strategic differentiation.

**How are lead referrals managed?**
Referrals are submitted via an official form (being migrated to PRM). The PRM will automate lead distribution to partners, eliminating manual tasks. The full historical leads database is tracked in a central spreadsheet. Wake also receives leads indicated by partners.

**How are partner incentives / sales campaigns structured?**
Wake runs sales campaigns ("corridas de vendas") for partners with rewards. The model is being unified across all partners — previously CRMBonus had a different reward structure. The goal is one standardized model for everyone.

**How are partner NFs (invoices) processed?**
NFs are emitted quarterly. The Parcerias team notifies when emission is open and accelerates payment within the same month where possible.

## Commercial & Pricing

**What is the WakeUP plan?**
WakeUP is an entry-level plan targeting agencies. Monthly fee starts at **R$ 1,000**. There was an open question about the fee for an additional store under this plan (showing as R$ 2,000). A FAQ doc for WakeUP was created internally to centralize doubts.

**What does a checkout migration typically cost?**
- Checkout only: from **R$ 5K** (ranging R$ 5K–15K depending on client size)
- Full store redesign: from **R$ 15K** (ranging R$ 15K–40K)
Migration demand typically falls to agency partners.

**What payment processing rates does Wake offer?**
Standard rate (all card brands, any installment count): **3.1%**, with payment on the receivables flow (not anticipated). Anticipated payment option available at **3.49%/month** on request. Includes anti-fraud and chargeback costs. Installments up to 12x.

**How does payment timing work (D+1)?**
D+1 is an advance — one business day after transaction approval. Wake's standard negotiation is on the receivables flow (not advanced). If a client pays in 1x credit, the merchant receives in 30 days; if in installments, it follows the installment schedule.

**Can Wake do B2B and B2C on the same environment?**
Yes. The same store supports both: public URL for B2C and a logged-in area for B2B with separate pricing tables, products, banners, and even different logos — all in one admin, one ERP integration.

**What is the Wake Gateway (GTW)?**
Wake has its own payment gateway. It supports acquirers like Rede (as an acquirer, not just a gateway). If a client needs Rede, clarify whether they need it as an acquirer or as a gateway — this is a common source of miscommunication during onboarding.

## Integrations & Technology

**Does Wake offer a sandbox/test environment?**
Wake does not provide a free test environment by default. If a sandbox is strictly necessary, a production environment must be provisioned and linked to the contract. This follows the same new-seller panel creation process.

**Which ERP integration partners are in Wake's ecosystem?**

**Does Wake integrate with Loggi and Melhor Envio?**
Yes, Wake has integrations with both Loggi and Melhor Envio.

**Does Wake integrate with Anymarket?**
Yes, with an active technical partnership. The Anymarket integration covers product catalog sync, inventory updates, order management, and marketplace listing. A technical "De/Para" (field mapping) guide exists for ERP teams to configure the integration correctly. Known issue: reserved stock from Wake is not currently consumed by Anymarket.

**What is Wake's Prateleira Infinita (Infinite Shelf / Connect)?**
An in-store product expansion feature. Important: not all POS terminals from Pagar.me are compatible. Clients interested in Prateleira Infinita must go through a specific commercial negotiation with Pagar.me to provision compatible terminals and a dedicated account.

**Can a client migrate their Pagar.me subscription base to Wake?**
If the client uses Pagar.me as a gateway for managing subscriptions, the subscription customer database migration is handled during platform migration. First step: identify whether the client is on Pagar.me's "Pronto para Usar (PSP)" or "Customizado (GTW Pagar.me)" plan, as the migration path differs.

## Support & Operations

**How does cross-support work between Wake and Anymarket?**
Issues are escalated via tickets on Anymarket's support portal (`suporte.anymarket.com.br`). Both CS teams tag each other in the relevant WhatsApp group with the ticket number. The Wake CS cross Slack channel (`@cscross`) covers all Wake products and includes team leads.

**What is the typical onboarding duration?**
Average is approximately **90 days**.

**How should partner-indicated leads be handled operationally?**
Leads are submitted via the official form (migrating to PRM). The flow generates a tracking task/reminder for the team to follow up with intention and increase conversion rate. Both Wake→partner and partner→Wake lead flows are tracked in the same system.

**Where should internal operational topics be discussed vs. WhatsApp groups?**
Long-running operational topics should be moved to **Slack** to avoid getting lost in WhatsApp. WhatsApp groups are for quick alignment and escalations; Slack threads are for structured resolution and documentation.
